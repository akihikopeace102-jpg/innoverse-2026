FAQs

Questions, answered.

Everything participants and schools most often ask before joining Innoverse.

Can students participate in multiple competitions?

Answer 01

No. Students may participate in only one event due to the event schedule and overlapping timelines.

Is an escort teacher from the school mandatory?

Answer 02

Yes. Each participating school must be accompanied by at least one escort teacher throughout the event.

Is there any participation fee?

Answer 03

No. Innoverse is completely free of cost for all participating schools.

Will lunch be provided to the participants?

Answer 04

Yes. Lunch will be provided to all participants throughout the three days. Food stalls will also be available, where participants can purchase additional refreshments.

Will participants receive any training before the competitions?

Answer 05

Yes. Participants will receive expert-led training and mentorship sessions to enhance their skills and prepare them for the competitions.

What should participants carry on the day of the event?

Answer 06

Participants must carry their school ID card and any event-specific materials mentioned in the event guidelines.
