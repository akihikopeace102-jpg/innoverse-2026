01 · About Innoverse

Innoverse is a student-led high-school Techfest and E-Summit hosted by Choithram School, Indore. Over three days, participants compete across ten events that test real skills — code, strategy, design, persuasion, and problem-solving under pressure.

Every event is built around a real scenario. Teams debug live systems, pitch to judges, negotiate through market crashes, or navigate obstacle courses blindfolded. There are no hypotheticals — only outcomes.

Innoverse is not a lecture series. It is a competition where preparation meets improvisation, and the difference between winning and losing is how well you adapt.
