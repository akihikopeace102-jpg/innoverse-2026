The world

Built around building machines.

From robotics and coding to engineering, design, and scientific innovation, Techfest offers hands-on experiences that encourage participants to build, collaborate, and push the boundaries of what's possible.

More than a competition, Techfest is a celebration of innovation. It's where ideas become prototypes, challenges become opportunities, and the innovators of tomorrow take their first step toward creating meaningful impact.

01

Techfest · Coding & Puzzle

Code Decode

The clock is ticking, the vault is locked, and every line of code brings you one step closer to the truth. CODE DECODE is a high-intensity coding adventure where technology, logic, and teamwork collide in an immersive heist mission.

Teams of 3–5 participants race against time to solve puzzles, write code, and deploy robots to uncover hidden clues scattered across the arena. As the mission intensifies, they must crack encrypted messages, disable digital alarms, and eliminate unexpected bugs before the vault seals forever.

Only the sharpest minds can beat the clock. The team that decodes the mystery, masters every challenge, and completes the mission with speed and precision will claim victory.

Teams of 3–5

02

Techfest · Robotics Racing

Frontier-1

When speed meets trust, every second counts. FRONTIER-1 is an adrenaline-fueled robotics racing challenge where communication is your greatest advantage and teamwork determines the winner.

Teams of 3-4 participants race wireless cars across an illuminated track, with one teammate blindfolded behind the controls while another guides them through ramps, traps, and barriers using only verbal instructions. Whether your car is self-built, kit-assembled, or store-bought, every machine is welcome, and dynamic obstacles keep every race unpredictable.

Crossing the finish line takes more than a fast car, it takes perfect coordination. Only the team that communicates with precision, adapts under pressure, and races with unwavering trust will emerge victorious.

Teams of 3–4

03

Techfest · Esports

The Crossfire

Gear up for the ultimate test of teamwork, strategy, and precision. The Crossfire is the flagship 5v5 VALORANT tournament where every match is a battle of skill, coordination, and tactical brilliance.

Teams of 5-6 participants compete through intense group-stage and knockout matches, relying on smart strategies, flawless communication, and split-second decisions to earn a place in the Best-of-3 Grand Finals.

Only one squad can conquer the battlefield. The team that outplays, outsmarts, and outlasts every opponent will be crowned the Crossfire Champions.

Teams of 5–6

04

Techfest · Crisis Management

Survivor

When the unexpected strikes, every decision becomes a turning point. Survivor is a high-intensity crisis management challenge where strategy, leadership, and resilience are the only way forward.

Teams of 4 participants take charge after a massive heist goes wrong, responding to real-time twists, negotiating with other teams, controlling the damage, and making critical decisions under pressure. The challenge culminates with teams presenting their final strategy before a panel of judges.

The crisis won't wait, and neither can you. Only the team that thinks faster, adapts smarter, and executes the most effective strategy will outlast the competition and emerge victorious.

Teams of 4

05

Techfest · Hardware & Innovation

Science Vault

Turn your ideas into innovation and let your creations do the talking. Science Vault is a hands-on hardware innovation challenge where creativity meets engineering to solve real-world problems.

Teams of 2–4 participants design and build a working hardware prototype, set up an interactive exhibition stall, and demonstrate how their solution works. Throughout the showcase, visitors cast live votes using physical tokens, with every vote contributing bonus points to the team's final score.

The greatest innovations are the ones that inspire. The team that delivers the most impactful solution, captivates the audience, and earns the highest score will claim the title of Science Vault Champion.

Teams of 2–4
